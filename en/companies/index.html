Corporate membership

A benefit your team actually uses.

  1. Tax-free benefit in kind

    In Germany, employers can grant employees benefits in kind of up to 50 euros a month tax-free. A corporate membership at COLORS can be structured to fit within this limit.

  2. A gym at the workplace

    Excellent equipment, well kept and built for independent training. Before work, during a break or afterwards, without a detour across the city.

  3. Network included

    The people training at COLORS work and live around Ostbahnhof. You get to know each other, beyond your own company.

  4. From ten employees

    Corporate terms are available from ten memberships. We discuss scope and price in person.

Note on the benefit in kind: the basis is the exemption limit of 50 euros per calendar month under Section 8(2) sentence 11 of the German Income Tax Act (EStG). It applies to all benefits in kind in a month combined and is subject to conditions. This is not tax advice; please clarify the details with your payroll or tax adviser.
